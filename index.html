Video Upscaling Handbook

Practical, vendor-neutral notes on AI video upscaling: how to diagnose a bad clip, what super resolution can and cannot do, and how to run the job at scale. Written for people who have footage to fix, not for people selling software.

  • How to Fix Pixelated Video: Compression Blocks, Banding and Jagged Edges

    Pixelation comes from three different causes. Learn which ones AI can repair, how to check a clip before you process it, and what to do with the result.

  • Video Super Resolution vs Resizing: What Actually Changes in the Frame

    Resizing and super resolution both produce a bigger frame, but only one adds detail. A plain-language explanation of how VSR uses neighbouring frames, and when it is worth it.

  • How to Batch Upscale Multiple Videos Without Wasting Credits

    A repeatable process for upscaling a folder of clips to one resolution: sort by source, test one, then run the batch. Includes a cost estimate worksheet.

  • Upscaling Video on a Mac Without Uploading It: When Local Beats Cloud

    Private footage, very long files and slow connections are the cases where on-device upscaling wins. What to expect from an Apple Silicon Mac, and when the cloud is still the better choice.

  • Automating Video Upscaling With a REST API: Submit, Poll, Download

    The pattern every video processing API shares, with idempotency keys, polling intervals and failure handling, illustrated with the VideoUpscaler API and its MCP server.

How these guides are written

Each guide starts from a concrete problem (blocks in a chat clip, forty wedding files, a two-hour recording that may not leave the building) and works through the decision before the tool. Where a specific product's numbers are quoted, they are taken from that product's own documentation at the time of writing. Nothing here is sponsored.
